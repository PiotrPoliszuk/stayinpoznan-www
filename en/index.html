A location that makes the stay

The Town Hall almost outside. The whole city within reach.

Wielka 23 sits by the north-eastern corner of the Old Market Square. Walk out for coffee, see the famous goats at noon and return on foot after dinner or a concert.

  • Old Market Square and Town Hall — around 100 m
  • Croissant Museum and the Fara — a short walk
  • Citadel Park and Cathedral Island — walkable
  • Poznań Expo and main station — easy by tram

Good to know

Questions before your stay

How close are the apartments to the Old Market Square?+

They are at Wielka 23, around 100 metres from the square and a few minutes on foot from the Town Hall.

How many guests can stay?+

Each apartment accommodates up to four guests, with a separate bedroom and a sofa bed in the living area.

Is there air conditioning and Wi-Fi?+

Yes. Both apartments offer air conditioning, fast Wi-Fi, a TV and an equipped kitchen.

Is there a lift?+

No. The apartments are on the fourth floor of a historic building dating from around 1900.
