Good to know before booking

The view comes with four flights of stairs.

The apartment is on the fourth floor of a historic building dating from around 1900. There is no lift, so it may not suit guests with limited mobility or very heavy luggage.

The Town Hall, famous goats, Croissant Museum, Fara church, restaurants and cafés are a short walk away. Citadel Park and Cathedral Island are walkable, while Poznań Expo is easy to reach by tram.
